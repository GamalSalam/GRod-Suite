Accuracy (vs Lufkin SROD)

  • Checked on 36 wells and speeds (4 of them blind).
  • Polished rod load: PPRL within 4%, MPRL within 7%.
  • Peak torque, conventional units: typically within 4% (RMS 3.4%, mean error -0.7%) on 14 SROD cases with manufacturer torque factors. Worst case +8.5%, at very low load (Fo/Skr below 0.15).
  • Net stroke within 8%. Rod loading (stress range as % of allowable) within about 3 points on six recent wells with 140,000 psi rods.
  • Polished rod HP typically within 5% (calibrated mode applies a 0.91 factor; pure RP 11L runs about 10% high). Power required within about 5% (worst 12%) on 14 wells.
  • Net and gross stroke (calibrated mode): pump load adjustment is added to the fluid load when reading the stroke chart, and the chart factor was refitted against 11 SROD runs (C912, 124 in). Within about 3% on most runs, worst about 6% (N/No' 0.24, Fo/Skr 0.29, 700 lb adjustment). Fo/Skr below 0.25 and above 0.36 are not yet verified.
  • Mark II and air-balanced torque are unverified.

Limits

  • Conventional units. Vertical wells, or deviated wells with a survey.
  • Not covered: gas interference, N/No above 0.5, Fo/Skr above 0.6. Deviated wells only with a survey (see Deviated wells).
  • Use for selection and screening. Confirm final designs with a full wave-equation run.
  • Unit ratings (C-456, C-640, C-912) are placeholders until your JSON is loaded.
  • Rod sizes you tick must form an API string (e.g. 7/8 + 3/4 gives 66, 77, 76).

Guided rods

  • Guided rods are set per taper: in Rod string & service factor, tick Custom rod string, then choose Normal, Guided (molded) or Guided (wheeled) for each taper. A guided taper is checked as its own taper and shown with "guided" after the size. Guided rods set from the survey suggestion override these choices until cleared. The sinker bar has its own Normal / Guided type (in section 2, and in the Custom rod string block).
  • Each guided section is split out of its taper and checked as its own taper, even when it is the same size as the rods around it. It is shown with "guided" after the size.
  • Without a survey, guides change nothing in the loads; only the taper split and quantities change. With a survey, guided rods get a higher friction coefficient (molded 1.5x, wheeled 0.5x the bare rod value). Guide weight is not modeled.
  • In the Design tab, with a survey and no kept guides, every option is run without guides, then rerun with the recommended guides. The Guided rods column shows how many rods; tap a row for the sections and to keep them.
  • With a survey, the Rod string section lists the rods above the bare-rod side-load limit with guides per rod: 3 molded up to about 140 lb/rod, 4 molded up to the wheeled limit in the survey block (default 150), wheeled above that. This follows SROD's pattern on two wells. It can fill the guided sections for you; edit them any time.

Deviated wells

  • Enter the survey as MD, INC, AZ per line (ft, deg, deg). TVD and dogleg come from minimum curvature.
  • Side load per rod = rod tension x dogleg angle over the rod length, plus a small weight term. Tension is the upstroke load profile with drag added from the bottom up.
  • Drag = friction coefficient x side load (0.2 bare rod, 1.5x molded, 0.5x wheeled). It raises the peak load, lowers the minimum load, and raises torque, counterbalance, rod stress and polished rod HP.
  • Net lift must be based on TVD at the pump, not MD. The survey box shows the TVD.
  • Checked on three SROD deviated wells: PPRL within 1%, MPRL within 5%, peak side load within 9% (slightly low), peak torque within 5%, polished rod HP within 6%. Guide positions: 141 of 149 and 30 of 32 SROD-flagged rods. Treat as approximate.
  • Dogleg is taken at the end station of each survey interval and interpolated between stations, which matches SROD's guide positions best. SROD's own smoothing still differs slightly at some depths.
  • Not modeled: buckling in compression on the downstroke. Air-balanced and Mark II: loads change but torque is not adjusted.

Torque

  • Conventional units use the manufacturer peak torque factor for the chosen stroke in place of S/2. Units without a stroke table use S/2.
  • The strokes offered depend on the selected unit.
  • The added-load factor (0.4) is fitted to SROD. A refit on 14 cases gave no real gain, so it is unchanged.
  • Counterbalance is assumed ideal; the real setting in SROD can differ.
  • Power required = polished rod HP x cyclic load factor / unit efficiency. Efficiency rises with unit loading (about 0.75 at 35% of the torque rating, 0.90 at 100%). The cyclic load factor defaults to 1.55, the SROD average for a balanced unit; it is higher when the counterbalance is off (SROD showed 1.8 to 2.7).
  • Gross plunger stroke is the stroke before tubing stretch; net stroke is after it. Pump displacement uses the net stroke.
  • Air-balanced and Mark II loads and torque follow your macro.

Rod loading columns

  • Grade D and High str.: maximum stress as % of the allowable (modified Goodman, with service factor), for Grade D or high-strength rods.
  • D range and HS range: stress range (max minus min) as % of the allowable range, for the same two materials.
  • Under 100% is OK. Over 100% is overloaded.
  • Custom string: sections can be in any order (a larger size can sit below a smaller one) and each can have its own grade. Set grade and Set range then show the loading at each section's own grade.

Inputs and defaults

  • Defaults are the SROD values: pump friction 200 lb, damping 0.05/0.15, stuffing box friction 100 lb.
  • Pump load adjustment and buoyant weight adjustment left blank use SROD depth scaling: 0.1 x depth and -0.05 x depth (500 and -250 lb at 5,000 ft). Type a number to override, or 0 to switch off.
  • Pump friction and load adjustment act at the pump (all tapers). Stuffing box friction acts at the surface only.
  • Damping is scaled down because in SROD runs it changes loads by under about 100 lb.
  • Net lift H should include tubing head pressure (SROD case: 4,885 ft).
  • Buoyancy factor 0.12 matches SROD (the API example uses 0.128).
  • Unanchored tubing stretch = Fo x Et x unanchored length (pump depth minus TAC depth) x factor (RP 11L uses 1.0; SROD runs suggest about 0.8).

Method

  • Charts come from your RP11L.xls (bilinear interpolation).
  • Corrected cells: F1 row N/No 0.15: 0.08, 0.08, 0.29 changed to 0.08, 0.18, 0.295. F3 Fo/Skr 0.4 column N/No 0.35 to 0.55 and (0.30, 0.5) re-estimated. F3 empty edges extrapolated.
  • Chart calibration multiplies the digitized F1, F2 and Sp/S values by factors fitted to 26 SROD runs (cross-validated: F1 error 4.2 to 3.0%, F2 14.2 to 9.5%, Sp/S 8.5 to 5.1%). Choose Pure RP 11L to switch it off.
  • Taper loads are true rod loads (hydrostatic pressure on the rod area removed, as SROD reports). The surface dynamic load is carried down each taper in proportion to (rod weight below / total)^0.75 on the upstroke (^1 for sinker bars) and ^0.5 on the downstroke, fitted to SROD wells (approximate).
  • Sinker bars: weight = 0.433 x SG x L x SBF / 0.978 (air weight; SBF for 2.0 in. interpolated; listed plunger sizes only). Bars shorten the lowest taper; Er and Fc unchanged.
